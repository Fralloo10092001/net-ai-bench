All devices lacked the required IPv4 addresses on their data-plane interfaces (eth1 and eth2). They were added according to the addressing plan. PC1 and PC2 were given specific subnet routes to reach each other's network via their local router. R1 and R2 already had IP forwarding enabled (net.ipv4.ip_forward = 1), so no change was needed. Static routes were added on the routers to forward traffic between the 10.0.1.0/24 and 10.0.2.0/24 subnets. Management interfaces and the existing default route via eth0 remain untouched.
