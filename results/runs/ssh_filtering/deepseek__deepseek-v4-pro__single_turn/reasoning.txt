The firewall 'fw' uses nftables with an inet filter table and a forward chain with a default drop policy. Currently only TCP port 2222 traffic is allowed. To permit port 22 from client to server, I added two nft rules: one allowing new and established connections from eth1 to eth2 with destination port 22, and one allowing established return traffic from eth2 to eth1 with source port 22. This keeps port 2222 working, leaves port 23 blocked, and does not alter management interfaces or flush state.
