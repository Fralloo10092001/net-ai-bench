The firewall already permits client-to-server traffic for TCP port 2222 and return traffic, with forward policy drop. To minimally enable SSH on port 22 without affecting management on eth0 or unrelated blocked ports like 23, I added two nftables forward-chain rules on fw: one to allow new and established TCP sessions from eth1 to eth2 with destination port 22, and one to allow established return traffic from eth2 to eth1 with source port 22. No other state was changed.
