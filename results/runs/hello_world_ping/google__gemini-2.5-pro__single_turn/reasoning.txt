The task is to enable connectivity between PC1 and PC2 through a router. PC1 and PC2 are on different subnets (10.0.1.0/24 and 10.0.2.0/24 respectively) and are already configured with the correct IP addresses and static routes pointing to the router as their gateway. The router, however, is missing the necessary IP addresses on its interfaces to serve as the gateway for these subnets. The solution adds the required IP addresses: 10.0.1.1/24 to eth1 (connected to PC1) and 10.0.2.1/24 to eth2 (connected to PC2). IP forwarding is already enabled on the router, so no configuration is needed for that. These changes establish the routing path between the two PCs.

Final validation result: passed 5/5 testcases.
