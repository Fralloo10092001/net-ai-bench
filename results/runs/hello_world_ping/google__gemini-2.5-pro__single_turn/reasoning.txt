The task is to enable connectivity between PC1 and PC2. PC1 (10.0.1.2/24) and PC2 (10.0.2.2/24) are on different subnets and are already configured with the correct IP addresses and static routes pointing to the router as their gateway for the remote subnet. The router, which connects these two subnets, needs IP addresses on its interfaces to act as the gateway. According to the topology, router's eth1 connects to PC1's network and eth2 connects to PC2's network. Therefore, IP address 10.0.1.1/24 is assigned to router's eth1, and 10.0.2.1/24 is assigned to router's eth2. The router's running configuration shows that IPv4 forwarding is already enabled, so no further action is needed for that. No changes are required on PC1 or PC2.
