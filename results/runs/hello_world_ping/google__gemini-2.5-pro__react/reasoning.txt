The task was to enable communication between PC1 and PC2. I inspected the topology and the router's configuration. I found that the router was missing the necessary IP addresses on its interfaces connected to the PCs. I added the IP address 10.0.1.1/24 to eth1 and 10.0.2.1/24 to eth2. IP forwarding was already enabled. After applying the configuration, I validated that PC1 could successfully ping PC2, which confirms the task is complete.
