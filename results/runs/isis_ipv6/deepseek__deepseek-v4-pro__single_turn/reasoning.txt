On r1 and r2, IPv6 forwarding is enabled via sysctl and FRR. The required global unicast addresses are added to the data-plane interfaces. The IS-IS process CORE is configured with wide metrics, a unique NET per router, and enabled on both data-plane interfaces. No changes are made to the management interfaces, and pc1/pc2 retain their existing static routes which provide reachability until IS-IS routes propagate, satisfying the requirement for dynamic IPv6 reachability.
