The planner intended to expose an internal HTTP service via a floating VIP (203.0.113.100/32) on edge2 eth1, with DNAT from TCP 203.0.113.100:8080 to 10.0.2.100:8080, MASQUERADE for symmetric return, and forward rules for both directions. The executor successfully completed: enabling IPv4 forwarding (already active), adding the VIP address (203.0.113.100/32) to eth1, verifying the route to 10.0.2.0/24 (already present), adding the DNAT rule in nat PREROUTING, and adding the MASQUERADE rule in nat POSTROUTING. These actions modified addresses and iptables but did not flush/reset iptables or alter existing management addressing. However, attempts to add the required iptables FORWARD rules (for new/established traffic to 10.0.2.100:8080 and for return traffic from 10.0.2.100:8080) were blocked by the environment, so no FORWARD rules were inserted. The validation step (HTTP request to 203.0.113.100:8080) was also blocked as a read-only command. Thus the DNAT and MASQUERADE components are configured, but the lack of matching FORWARD rules may prevent traffic from being forwarded, and end-to-end connectivity has not been verified.
